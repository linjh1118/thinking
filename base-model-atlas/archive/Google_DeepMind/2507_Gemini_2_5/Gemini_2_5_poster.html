Model Family & Motivation

Core Vision

Gemini 2.5 unifies RL-trained Thinking with native multimodality and 1M+ token long context — covering the full Pareto frontier from daily tasks to complex coding.

Gemini 1.5 established long context (1M tokens) and native multimodality, but had limited reasoning. Gemini 2.5's core goal: trade extra computation for higher accuracy at inference time.

Model Architecture

  • Sparse MoE — Sparse Mixture-of-Experts Transformer
  • Native Multimodal — unified text, visual, audio inputs
  • Long Context — 1M token context window
  • Distillation — k-sparse approximation for Flash & smaller models

Thinking Mechanism

Trained with RL, spending tens of thousands of forward-pass steps "thinking" before answering.

  • Dynamic Thinking Budget — user-controllable thinking token allocation
  • Consistent Scaling — more thinking → higher accuracy
  • Multimodal Integration — thinking works across images, video, audio
  • Agentic — thinking interleaves with Google Search tool calls
