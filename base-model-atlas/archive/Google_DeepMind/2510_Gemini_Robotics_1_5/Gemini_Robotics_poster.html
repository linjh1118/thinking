Problem: Toward Truly General Robots

Truly general robots require three capabilities simultaneously:

  • Deep physical world understanding: spatial reasoning, temporal reasoning, intuitive physics, causal relationships, affordances
  • Advanced reasoning: decompose complex multi-step tasks, adapt plans during execution
  • Generalization: work across different robot morphologies without per-robot fine-tuning
Core Motivation: Bring Thinking to the Physical World

GR 1.0 established foundations but lacked explicit thinking and cross-embodiment generalization. GR 1.5 integrates Gemini's Thinking capability into robotic VLA.

Motion Transfer (MT) Mechanism

Core innovation: learn from heterogeneous, multi-embodiment robot data to form unified motion and physics understanding.

Multi-Embodiment Learning
ALOHA + Bi-arm Franka + Apollo humanoid trained jointly. MT mechanism aligns common motion patterns across embodiments while preserving robot-specific control.
Data Sufficiency Effect on MT
  • Data-rich (ALOHA): MT aligns & extracts cross-embodiment commonalities
  • Data-medium (Bi-arm Franka): MT significantly amplifies positive transfer from other robots
  • Data-scarce (Apollo humanoid): Cross-robot data brings largest benefit; MT effect more limited

Thinking VLA: Interleaved Reasoning + Action

Thinking-Action Interleaving
Observation → Think(language) → Action → Observation → Think(language) → Action → ...
  • Task Decomposition: Converts high-level instructions into concrete short-horizon steps
  • Self-Correction: Detects failures → proposes recovery behaviors autonomously
  • Interpretability: Visualize robot's thought trajectory — builds human trust
  • Context Awareness: Implicit sub-task success sensing (e.g., grasped ball → switch target)

Two-stage: (1) Generate language thinking trace to convert complex task to short-horizon commands; (2) Map commands directly to robot actions.

GR-ER 1.5: Embodied Reasoning Capabilities

Complex Pointing

Predicts point sets rather than single points → enables motion trajectories, paths, spatial descriptions (e.g., "point to the space left of the cup"). Uses Gemini's Thinking for spatial reasoning.

Progress Understanding

Predicts task completion percentage (0-100%), detects success/failure at 5Hz in real-time. Works with single-view or multi-view cameras, online or offline.

Thinking Scaling

Harder tasks benefit from longer thinking (peaks 3072 tokens for QA vs 2048 for pointing). Model auto-allocates thinking budget per task difficulty.
