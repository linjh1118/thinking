👁 Native Multimodal Training

Unlike previous Llama models that added vision post-hoc, Llama 4 trains on text and images jointly from the beginning using a unified transformer architecture.

Early Fusion — Key Differentiator
Images and text are fused at the input layer into a unified sequence, rather than using a separate vision encoder with cross-attention. This early fusion enables deeply aligned multimodal representations from the start of pre-training.
• MetaCLIP-based visual encoder
• Interleaved image-text pre-training
• Variable-resolution image support
• Multi-image understanding in single context
Why Native Multimodal Matters
Joint training allows the model to develop grounded representations where visual and textual concepts are natively aligned, enabling superior visual reasoning vs. post-hoc fusion.
🎓 Training: Behemoth → Scout/Maverick

Behemoth serves as a teacher model for distilling capabilities into Scout and Maverick, enabling smaller models to acquire complex reasoning beyond their parameter count.

1
Pre-training — Joint text + image pre-training on massive multimodal corpus
2
Distillation — Behemoth teacher → Scout/Maverick students
3
Post-training — SFT + RLHF for instruction following + safety
4
Long-context Training — Scout extends to 10M token context via iRoPE
