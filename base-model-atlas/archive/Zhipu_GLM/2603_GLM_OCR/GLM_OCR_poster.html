Problem: Large MLLMs Are Overkill for OCR

Large MLLMs (235B+) achieve strong document understanding but suffer from prohibitive compute costs. Core insight: OCR is a structured, locally-deterministic task — token-by-token autoregressive decoding is unnecessary overkill.

Key Insight

OCR is deterministic, structured, and locally-dependent. MTP (Multi-Token Prediction) can predict multiple tokens in parallel, dramatically improving efficiency.
