As multimodal understanding, instruction following, and complex task execution keep improving, video models will be able to grasp fuller creative intent, handle more complex content needs, and gradually move from &quot;generating a clip&quot; to genuinely participating in the entire content production process.</p><h3 class="text-xl font-semibold mb-3 mt-5">H3&#x27;s Technical Choices</h3><p class="mb-4 leading-relaxed">H3 is built on a simple design philosophy, but bringing it to life was extraordinarily complex. From Hailuo 01 and Hailuo 02 to H3, each generation has been an order of magnitude more complex to build than the last.</p><p class="mb-4 leading-relaxed">A brief look at some of H3&#x27;s core technologies:</p><ul class="list-disc pl-6 mb-4 space-y-1"><li class="leading-relaxed">H3-Contextual Omni Representation</li></ul><p class="mb-4 leading-relaxed">One of the most important things we did in engineering H3 was strengthening its captioning capability.</p><ul class="list-disc pl-6 mb-4 space-y-1"><li class="leading-relaxed">Introducing multimodal context further broadened what &quot;caption&quot; needs to cover, we&#x27;re not just describing the target video anymore, but the relationship between context and target video, and even relationships among elements within the context itself.</li><li class="leading-relaxed">We need to jointly describe video and audio, and that audio-visual relationship gets even more complex across multiple shots.</li><li class="leading-relaxed">This is fundamentally a form of Contextual Omni Representation, where language acts as the generalizable bridge and interpreter, unifying &quot;tasks&quot; into an open, descriptive form. This is the root of H3&#x27;s broad instruction-following ability.</li><li class="leading-relaxed">To get there, we built dedicated models and a full-modality understanding pipeline. Most source material requires around 100K tokens of inference, distilled down to an average of roughly 4K tokens.</li><li class="leading-relaxed">H3-VAE: A Major Boost in Architectural Efficiency</li><li class="leading-relaxed">The H series has continuously pushed forward on tokenizer technology. With H3, we completely overhauled our previous tokenizer, achieving across-the-board gains in reconstruction quality and learnability, giving H3 a competitive edge in efficiency. Its high compression ratio also delivers a 4x gain in effective sequence length, substantially cutting training and inference costs, and it&#x27;s the key technology behind our native 2K resolution support.</li><li class="leading-relaxed">H3-Omni Transformer</li></ul><p class="mb-4 leading-relaxed">H3-Omni Transformer: An Architecture Built for Task Generalization</p><ul class="list-disc pl-6 mb-4 space-y-1"><li class="leading-relaxed">In keeping with H3&#x27;s design philosophy that &quot;architecture should serve the task,&quot; generality and efficiency were the only two goals. Notably, we set aside the Hailuo-02 architecture, despite the significant architectural advantages it once gave us, because it would introduce unnecessary complexity for a model built around task generalization. We believe task generalization is an irreversible trend, and architectural tricks should give way to how the model is defined.</li><li class="leading-relaxed">In H3, the introduction of multimodal context tripled the variance in sequence length, and the compute workloads for understanding and generation became markedly more heterogeneous. We adopted a training architecture that separates understanding and generation workloads, fine-tuning hardware utilization for each, while jointly balancing per-sample heterogeneous compute against load balancing across samples. End to end, this lifted training throughput by nearly 30%.</li><li class="leading-relaxed">H3-In-context Regeneration</li><li class="leading-relaxed">For H3&#x27;s 2K output, instead of using a conventional dedicated super-resolution module, we have the H3 base model regenerate its own low-resolution output in-context. This brings two advantages: first, the regeneration process maximally reuses the generative capability already built into the H3 base model; second, the in-context approach lets it draw on the original multimodal context again to produce high-resolution output, recovering details that traditional super-resolution can only &quot;guess&quot; at and often can&#x27;t restore, like small text and fine detail.</li></ul><p class="mb-4 leading-relaxed">We&#x27;ll be sharing the full H3 Technical Report soon.
