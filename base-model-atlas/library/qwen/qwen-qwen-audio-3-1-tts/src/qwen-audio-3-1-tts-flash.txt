Qwen-Audio-3.1-TTS-Flash is a high-performance text-to-speech model designed for real-time interactions. It supports multiple languages and dialects, streaming speech synthesis, free-style instruction following, and fine-grained tag control for emotion, tone, persona, speaking rate, and volume. In voice cloning scenarios, it is more robust to noise and reverberation in reference audio, with improved audio quality, clarity, and expressiveness. The Flash version is optimized for low-latency applications such as voice assistants, real-time conversations, and customer service.
