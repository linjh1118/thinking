Intended Task/Domain:                                                                   |  Basketball play-level multimodal Q&A, including fine-grained visual recognition (player actions, shot mechanics, shot outcomes, rebounding), play-structure and possession reasoning, player identification (jersey numbers and team colours), and open-ended play description and structured captioning over broadcast footage.
Model Type:                                                                                            |  Transformer
Intended Users:                                                                                        |  Basketball broadcast and media teams, coaching and performance analysts, sports analytics and production workflows, and enterprise developers building basketball-domain search and assistant applications over archived game video.
Output:                                                                                                |  Text responses to basketball-domain questions over play-level video inputs (with broadcast audio when available), including structured multiple-choice answers and open-ended Q&A; supports chain-of-thought reasoning.
Describe how the model works:                                                                          |  The model combines three encoders: a Nemotron Nano V3 LLM backbone (31B MoE, ~3B active), a CRADIO v4-H vision encoder, and a Parakeet speech encoder. Video frames from a play clip are encoded into visual tokens, audio is encoded via the Parakeet encoder when present, and all modality tokens are concatenated with the text question before being processed by the language model to produce a text answer.

Technical Limitations & Mitigation:                                                                    |  This model supports English only and is optimized for play-level basketball video clips rather than full-game or long-form video. Video input is sampled at 2 FPS (max 32 frames); fine-tuning clips were under 40 seconds. Standalone speech transcription, document OCR, and general-purpose agentic workflows are out of scope for this fine-tuned release. Jersey-number identification may degrade when numbers are small, distant, or occluded in broadcast footage. Performance may vary on non-basketball content, low-quality or heavily occluded footage, noisy audio, and across different GPU configurations and precision formats. Demographic parity testing across protected classes was not conducted for this fine-tuned basketball release.

Potential Known Risks:                                                                                 |  The model may generate incorrect or hallucinated answers when processing ambiguous play footage, occluded players, small or unreadable jersey numbers, or low-quality video.
