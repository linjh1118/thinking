Output:                                                                                                |  Text responses to tennis-domain questions over point-level video inputs (with audio when available), including structured multiple-choice answers and open-ended Q&A; supports chain-of-thought reasoning.
Describe how the model works:                                                                          |  The model combines three encoders: a Nemotron Nano V3 LLM backbone (31B MoE, ~3B active), a CRADIO v4-H vision encoder, and a Parakeet speech encoder. Video frames from a point clip are encoded into visual tokens, audio is encoded via the Parakeet encoder when present, and all modality tokens are concatenated with the text question before being processed by the language model to produce a text answer.

Technical Limitations & Mitigation:                                                                    |  This model supports English only and is optimized for point-level tennis video clips rather than full-match or long-form video. Video input is limited to under 2 minutes at 1 FPS (max 128 frames). Standalone speech transcription, document OCR, and general-purpose agentic workflows are out of scope for this fine-tuned release. Performance may vary on non-tennis content, low-quality or heavily occluded footage, noisy audio, and across different GPU configurations and precision formats. Demographic parity testing across protected classes was not conducted for this fine-tuned tennis release.
