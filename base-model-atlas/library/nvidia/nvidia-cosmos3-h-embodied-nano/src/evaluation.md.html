Cosmos3-H-Embodied-Nano: Quantitative Evaluation

This document describes the quantitative evaluation of Cosmos3-H-Embodied-Nano:

  • Forward dynamics over long rollouts (action-conditioned video prediction, chunk by chunk),

implemented in scripts/quant_eval.py;

  • All three modes on single clips (forward dynamics, inverse dynamics, policy), with

scripts/eval.py (below);

  • A benchmark against Cosmos-H-Surgical-Simulator,

a forward-dynamics-only model, so it compares the one mode both models have, on episodes held out by both. The protocol and the metrics (FDS, GATC, TCD) follow that repository's evaluation; the benchmark adds FID and FVD, and 31.2 s rollouts on CMR Versius.

Overview

For each selected episode the script:

  1. Generates a video autoregressively from a start frame of the episode (its first frame, or

the first one with a valid sample window) and the recorded actions. Each chunk predicts 24 frames at 10 fps (2.4 s) and is conditioned on the last generated frame of the previous chunk. Through the dataset sample, the model also receives the 16 actions before the chunk (recorded, as in deployment, where the executed actions are known). The default of 3 chunks gives 72 generated frames (7.2 s), the horizon of Cosmos-H-Surgical-Simulator's evaluation (6 chunks of 12 frames).

  1. Scores the generated video against the recorded one with four metrics, over all generated

frames and per phase.

  1. Aggregates the scores per dataset and overall (summary.json, summary.md).

Episodes come from the held-out split (--dataset.dataset-split val, the 5% of episodes per dataset that training did not see), or from a plan file listing episodes and start frames. The commands below use 35 sampling steps and guidance 6.0 (--num-steps, --guidance), as the evaluation in the main README; each episode is rolled out with 2 seeds by default.

Metrics

Frames are compared in \([-1, 1]\) (\(x \mapsto x / 127.5 - 1\)). The conditioning frame (\(t = 0\)) is never scored. Let \(G_t\) and \(\hat{G}_t\) be the recorded and generated frames, \(t = 1, \ldots, T\).

MetricDefinitionDirection
FDS (Frame Decay Score)Mean per-frame L1: \(\frac{1}{T} \sum_t \frac{1}{3HW} \sum_{i,j,c} \lvert \hat{G}_t - G_t \rvert\), as in Cosmos-H-Surgical-SimulatorLower is better
SSIMMean per-frame SSIM (scikit-image, data range 2)Higher is better
PSNR\(20 \log_{10}(2 / \sqrt{\mathrm{MSE}})\), MSE over all generated framesHigher is better
Dynamic PSNRPSNR over the pixels whose recorded frame-to-frame change \(\lvert G_t - G_{t-1} \rvert\) (channel mean) is in the top 20% of the clipHigher is better

Dynamic PSNR is the PSNR of what moves: in endoscopic video most pixels are tissue that barely changes, so PSNR alone rewards a model that copies the conditioning frame. The motion mask is taken from the recorded video only (cosmos_framework.inference.metrics.compute_dynamic_psnr).

Each metric is also reported for three phases, the chunks of Cosmos-H-Surgical-Simulator's evaluation:

PhaseFramesTime after the conditioning frame
Early1–120.1–1.2 s
Mid13–361.3–3.6 s
Late37–723.7–7.2 s

Tool metrics

The benchmark below also reports the two tool metrics of Cosmos-H-Surgical-Simulator, computed by tools/benchmark/tool_metrics.py (a port of its implementation). Both need a surgical tool segmenter: Medical-SAM3 fine-tuned on CholecSeg8k, prompted with "surgical tool" (instances with score ≥ 0.3). It is trained on laparoscopic video, so these metrics are reported for CMR Versius only, not for the dVRK datasets.

MetricDefinitionDirection
GATC (GT-Anchored Tool Consistency)Per frame: zero-normalized cross-correlation of the grayscale recorded and generated frames inside the recorded tool mask (dilated by 10 px), maximized over shifts of ±3 px and ±1 frame, times a tool-presence factor (generated / recorded gradient energy in the mask, at most 1). Median over the frames with a tool. In [-1, 1]Higher is better
TCD (Tool Centroid Distance)Per frame: centroids of the tool instances in the recorded and generated frames, matched by the Hungarian algorithm; a recorded tool without a match costs half the image diagonal. Median over the frames with a tool, in pixelsLower is better

TCD mixes two errors: tools that are misplaced and tools that the segmenter does not find in the generated frame. tool_metrics.py also reports them separately (mean distance of the matched tools, share of recorded tools left unmatched).

Realism metrics

FID and FVD compare the distribution of generated frames or clips with that of the recorded ones, not each prediction with its own recording; they are computed by tools/benchmark/realism_metrics.py on the frames the benchmark scores (conditioning frame excluded).

MetricDefinitionDirection
FID (Fréchet Inception Distance)Fréchet distance between Gaussians fitted to the Inception-v3 pool features (TensorFlow FID weights, via torch-fidelity) of single generated and recorded framesLower is better
FVD (Fréchet Video Distance)The same with I3D (Kinetics-400) features of 16-frame clips taken every 8 frames, resized to 224×224Lower is better
Sharpness ratioMean Sobel gradient energy of the generated frames / that of the recorded frames1 is ideal; below 1: blurrier
Motion ratioMean frame-to-frame change \(\lvert \hat{G}_t - \hat{G}_{t-1} \rvert\) of the generated video / that of the recorded video1 is ideal

FID and FVD depend on the number of samples, so models are only compared on the same rollouts. The confidence intervals come from a paired bootstrap over clips (200 resamples). FID measures how realistic single frames look; FVD also measures motion.

Usage

All modes on held-out clips

scripts/eval.py runs each mode on single 2.4 s clips (25 frames, 24 actions) and scores it against the recording: forward dynamics (video from the first frame and the recorded actions), inverse dynamics (actions from the recorded video) and policy (actions and video from the first frame, the prompt and the action history).

  • Clips: 64 per family, evenly spaced over its val split (--spread-samples), for the 10

public families with held-out episodes (dvrk_rob_surgical has none).

  • Metrics: PSNR of the generated clip (all 25 frames, the conditioning frame included);

action MSE over the 24 predicted rows in normalized action space (each dimension mapped to [-1, 1] by the family's 1st/99th percentiles).

  • Policy, 4 rollouts per clip (seeds 0–3), since its output is multimodal (the Cosmos 3 paper

reports policy mode over 4 rollouts for the same reason): the mean, and the best of 4 (lowest MSE, highest PSNR, per clip).

  • Baseline: predicting the mean recorded chunk of the family's 64 clips for every clip (its

MSE is the variance of the actions). It is taken from the evaluated clips themselves, so it is an optimistic constant predictor.

  • One seed for forward and inverse dynamics; 35 steps, guidance 6.0.

  • Policy: the mean of 4 rollouts is 31% below the baseline, the best of 4 56%. The mean is

3.1 dB closer to the recording than the video generated with the policy's own actions, on 9 of 10 families, consistent with the video following the actions it is conditioned on.

  • MSE in this normalized space is not comparable across papers or action representations.

Benchmark: Cosmos-H-Surgical-Simulator

Both models are rolled out from the same frames with the same recorded actions, and scored against the same recorded frames.

Protocol

Resolution256 tier: 320×176 for 16:9 video512×288 (2.6× the pixels)
Frame rate10 fps10 fps (per-embodiment frame interval)
  • Episodes held out by both models: in the 5% validation split of this model's training

data and in the trailing 5% test split of Cosmos-H-Surgical-Simulator (episodes entirely in it). These sets barely overlap (one is random, the other the last episodes), which limits the benchmark to the episodes below.

  • Same start, same frames: each rollout starts at the episode's first frame valid for both

models' sample filters (usually frame 0) and predicts the same source frames.

  • Horizon: CMR Versius 7.2 s (72 frames, Cosmos-H-Surgical-Simulator's evaluation horizon);

dVRK 4.8 s (48 frames), since many held-out dVRK episodes are shorter than 7.2 s (17 usable episodes instead of 9).

  • Datasets: all held-out-by-both episodes of the 4 CMR Versius procedures (64), and of the

dVRK datasets on which both models were trained and for which Cosmos-H-Surgical-Simulator's normalization statistics are published (17: Hamlyn, Stanford, UCSD, UC Berkeley, Turin). JHU datasets are excluded: the Hugging Face dataset does not include Cosmos-H-Surgical-Simulator's statistics for them. The episode list is tools/benchmark/chss_benchmark_plan.json.

  • 2 seeds per episode: 128 CMR and 34 dVRK rollouts per model.
  • Scoring (compare_rollouts.py): the reference

frames are decoded from the Open-H Hugging Face videos, cropped to the part of the source frame both models generate and resized to 176 rows (antialiased bilinear), this model's output height; each prediction is cropped and resized the same way, so Cosmos-H-Surgical-Simulator's output is downscaled. Paired differences over rollouts: mean with 95% confidence interval, win rate, and Wilcoxon signed-rank p-value. The same scoring at 256 rows is a sensitivity check (below).

episode indices of each procedure (Cosmos-H-Surgical-Simulator's test split). Inference settings are each model's released defaults, not tuned on these episodes.

Reproducing

bash
# Cosmos3-H-Embodied-Nano (this repository; one process per GPU and family, see Usage)
python -m cosmos3_h_embodied.scripts.quant_eval --no-guardrails --checkpoint-path <dcp dir> \
    --config-file cosmos3_h_embodied/configs/config.py --experiment openh_h16_task_prompt \
    --dataset.dataset cmr_clinical --dataset.dataset-split full \
    --plan tools/benchmark/chss_benchmark_plan.json --num-seeds 2 --seed 0 --num-steps 35 --guidance 6.0 \
    -o outputs/benchmark/c3hen
# Repeat for dvrk_stanford, dvrk_hamlyn, dvrk_ucsd, dvrk_ucberkeley, turin_mitic.

# Cosmos-H-Surgical-Simulator (from its checkout and environment)
PYTHONPATH=. python <this repo>/tools/benchmark/chss_rollout.py \
    --experiment cosmos_predict2p5_2B_action_conditioned_open_h-fixed_13frame_8nodes_release_oss \
    --ckpt_path model_ema_bf16.safetensors --plan <this repo>/tools/benchmark/chss_benchmark_plan.json \
    --data_root <Open-H>/Surgical --output <this repo>/outputs/benchmark/chss --num_seeds 2

# Scoring (this repository)
python tools/benchmark/compare_rollouts.py --data-root <Open-H>/Surgical \
    --groups tools/benchmark/chss_benchmark_groups.json --height 176 \
    --rollouts c3hen=outputs/benchmark/c3hen chss=outputs/benchmark/chss -o outputs/benchmark/results

# Tool metrics, CMR Versius (GPU; one process per GPU with --num-shards N --shard-index i,
# then --summarize-only with the same --rollouts and -o)
python tools/benchmark/tool_metrics.py --data-root <Open-H>/Surgical \
    --sam3-repo <Medical-SAM3 checkout> --sam3-checkpoint <Medical-SAM3 fine-tuned on CholecSeg8k> --height 176 \
    --rollouts c3hen=outputs/benchmark/c3hen chss=outputs/benchmark/chss -o outputs/benchmark/tool_metrics

# FID, FVD (GPU; sharded like the tool metrics). Weights: torch-fidelity's
# weights-inception-2015-12-05-6726825d.pth (its v0.2.0 release) and StyleGAN-V's i3d_torchscript.pt
python tools/benchmark/realism_metrics.py --data-root <Open-H>/Surgical --groups tools/benchmark/chss_benchmark_groups.json \
    --inception-weights weights-inception-2015-12-05-6726825d.pth --i3d-weights i3d_torchscript.pt \
    --rollouts c3hen=outputs/benchmark/c3hen chss=outputs/benchmark/chss -o outputs/benchmark/realism

# Long rollouts: both rollout commands above with --plan tools/benchmark/chss_benchmark_plan_long.json
# (its num_frames sets the horizon), then realism_metrics.py and tool_metrics.py with --window-frames 60
