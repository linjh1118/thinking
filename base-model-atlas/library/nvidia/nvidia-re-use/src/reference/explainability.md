# Explainability Subcard

## Intended Task/Domain:

Speech processing

## Model Type:

Mamba

## Intended Users:

Researchers and general users can use this model to enhance the quality of speech recordings across a wide range of applications.

## Output:

Enhanced speech signal

## Describe how the model works:

The model maps the noisy input to clean output speech.

## Name the adversely impacted groups this has been tested to deliver comparable outcomes regardless of:

Not Applicable

## Technical Limitations and Mitigation:

The restoration may not be always accurate, and the model's performance may vary depending on the type and severity of the speech degradation.

## Verified to have met prescribed NVIDIA quality standards:

Yes

## Performance Metrics:

Speech quality metrics (PESQ, STOI, UTMOS, etc.)

## Potential Known Risks:

If the speech degradation is too severe, the model may not be able to effectively restore the quality of the input speech.
