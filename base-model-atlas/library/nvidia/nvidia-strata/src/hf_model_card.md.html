Earth-2 Checkpoints: STRATA

Description:

STRATA (Storm-resolving Tile-based autoRegressive Atmosphere Transformer Architecture) is a global, kilometer-scale AI atmosphere model that emulates the SCREAM (Simple Cloud-Resolving E3SM Atmosphere Model) global storm-resolving simulation. It autoregressively predicts the three-dimensional atmospheric state on SCREAM's native ne1024pg2 cubed-sphere grid (6 faces × 2048 × 2048 ≈ 25.2 million columns, ~4.9 km grid spacing) with a 10-minute time step. The prognostic state consists of potential temperature, zonal and meridional wind, geopotential height, vertical pressure velocity and specific humidity on 24 model levels, plus 2-m air temperature; surface pressure and liquid/ice surface precipitation fluxes are diagnosed at every step.

Inference is tile based: the global state is processed as overlapping cubed-sphere tiles (128–256 pixels with halos), so a single GPU can advance any part of the globe and a multi-GPU domain decomposition advances the full globe. Training recipes and the inference wrapper are in the public NVlabs/STRATA repository (Apache-2.0); the network architecture itself lives in NVIDIA PhysicsNeMo as physicsnemo.experimental.models.strata.

This model is ready for commercial or non-commercial use.

Global

Use Case:

Kilometer-scale global atmosphere emulation for weather and climate research: short-range (hours to days) forecasting of the SCREAM state, emulation of storm-resolving simulations, and research on coupling AI emulators of different resolutions (e.g. large-scale nudging / downscaling experiments).

Model Architecture:

Architecture Type: Two-stage transformer ("PixelDiT"): a semantic transformer backbone operating on 4×4-pixel patches (24 blocks, width 1024, 16 heads, 3-D neighborhood attention with kernel 9 and dilation 3, gated attention, alternating depth-wise attention, stereographic 2-D rotary position embeddings, latitude/longitude index conditioning, wind-vector rotation across cube faces) followed by a pixel-resolution refinement transformer (4 blocks, width 128, kernel 9, bilinear depth-wise GELU projection with adaptive layer norm). The model is a deterministic single-step predictor trained with a regression (smooth-L1) loss on a mixed state/tendency target; no diffusion sampling is involved. 331.7 M parameters.

Network Architecture: Transformer with Neighborhood Attention (NATTEN)

This model provides the following checkpoint:

  • checkpoints/strata_v1.pth -- ne1024pg2 grid, 10-minute time step, 331.7 M parameters (pretraining followed by a 4-step autoregressive fine-tune)

Input:

Input Type(s):

  • Tensor (149 channels: 145 prognostic atmospheric state channels and 4 forcing channels)

Input Format(s): PyTorch tensors

Input Parameters:

  • Four Dimensional (4D): (batch, channel, tile height, tile width) per cubed-sphere tile; the tiled inference wrapper accepts the global state as (batch, channel, 6 faces, 2048, 2048)

Other Properties Related to Input:

  • Grid: SCREAM ne1024pg2 cubed-sphere physics grid (6 × 2048 × 2048 columns).
  • Prognostic 3-D variables on 24 SCREAM hybrid model levels (archived level indices 8–31; nominal pressures ≈100, 117, 136, 158, 184, 214, 250, 290, 338, 394, 459, 534, 614, 690, 758, 814, 854, 880, 904, 927, 948, 967, 983 and 998 hPa for a 1000 hPa surface): PotentialTemperature, U, V, z_mid, omega, qv.
  • Prognostic 2-D variable: T_2m.
  • Forcing channels: coszr (cosine of the solar zenith angle, computed from the valid time of the atmospheric state before the tensor is assembled), phis (surface geopotential), sgh30 (sub-grid orography standard deviation), landfrac (land fraction). The three static fields are distributed with the model as phis.npy, sgh30.npy and landfrac.npy.
  • Inputs are normalized per channel with statistics stored in the checkpoint (also exported as input_norm_mean.npy / input_norm_var.npy; channel order in channels.json).

Output:

Output Type(s): Tensor (148 channels: the 145 prognostic state channels at t + 10 min and 3 diagnostic channels)

Output Format: PyTorch Tensors

Output Parameters: Four Dimensional (4D) (batch, channel, tile height, tile width)

Other Properties Related to Output:

  • Diagnostic channels: ps (surface pressure), precip_liq_surf_mass_flux, precip_ice_surf_mass_flux (surface precipitation mass fluxes, m/s)
  • Output lead time is one 10-minute step; longer forecasts are produced autoregressively by the wrapper.

Training, Testing, and Evaluation Datasets:

Training Dataset:

Link: STRATA-SCREAM-sdecadal, STRATA-SCREAM-sdy1, STRATA-SCREAM-sdy2

Data Collection Method by dataset:

  • Automated (numerical simulation output)

Labeling Method by dataset:

  • Not Applicable (self-supervised next-step prediction; no human labels)

Data Modality:

  • Gridded geophysical time series

Data Size:

  • ~76 TB total (sdecadal 36.8 TB, sdy1 19.6 TB, sdy2 19.7 TB); the training periods below are subsets of each store

Properties: Output of three SCREAM v1 global storm-resolving simulations on the ne1024pg2 grid (~4.9 km physics grid), archived every 10 minutes on 32 model levels and published as cubed-sphere zarr stores: a 14-day segment of a transient historical (F20TR) simulation (sdecadal, 1994-10-01 to 1994-10-14) and two 7-day present-day (F2010) simulations for the DYAMOND periods (sdy1: 2016-08-01 to 2016-08-07; sdy2: 2020-01-20 to 2020-01-26). Training uses 17 simulated days: 1994-10-02 to 1994-10-10 of sdecadal, 2016-08-02 to 2016-08-05 of sdy1 and 2020-01-21 to 2020-01-24 of sdy2 (the first simulated day of every store is excluded; the remaining days of sdecadal are held out for testing and evaluation). The published sdecadal zarr store and the model code label the sdecadal dates as October 2020. The datasets are released under CC-BY-4.0.

Testing Dataset:

Link: same simulations as above (held-out later time periods of each simulation)

Data Collection Method by dataset:

  • Automated (numerical simulation output)

Labeling Method by dataset:

  • Not Applicable

Properties: Held-out portion of the sdecadal simulation after the training period (simulated days 11–14; validation samples every 6 hours on day 14), never seen during training (dates labelled October 2020 in the store).

Evaluation Dataset:

Link: same simulations as above

Data Collection Method by dataset:

  • Automated (numerical simulation output)

Labeling Method by dataset:

  • Not Applicable

Properties: Multi-day autoregressive rollouts initialized from six held-out sdecadal states at 12-hourly intervals (2020-10-11 12:00 to 2020-10-14 00:00 UTC in the store's date labelling) are scored against the SCREAM trajectory: area-weighted RMSE and bias per variable and level, radially averaged power spectra, and precipitation statistics; see the paper for results.

Inference:

Engine: PyTorch, PhysicsNeMo

Test Hardware:

  • H100
  • A100
