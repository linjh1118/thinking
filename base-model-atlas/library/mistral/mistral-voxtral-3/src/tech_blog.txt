Summary
Voxtral 3 introduces a cutting-edge voice agent SDK and state-of-the-art speech models designed for real-time, multi-step conversations across 50+ languages. It combines accurate transcription, natural speech synthesis with emotional adaptability, and deep tool integration for seamless workflow automation. Available via Mistral’s EU-hosted API or self-deployment, it ensures data sovereignty while delivering low-latency, human-like interactions for contact centers, virtual assistants, and hands-free copilots.
Speech transcription and voice generation have come a long way, but building end-to-end voice agents that can listen, reason, interact, and handle complex workflows remains difficult.
A reliable voice agent needs to do four things very well (and simultaneously):
Listen accurately
across languages, accents, speakers, and interruptions,
Reason
deeply
about what users mean in specific contexts,
Speak naturally
with emotion and expression, and
Take action
over multiple steps through connected systems.
Getting all four to work together reliably in real time remains a challenge.
To address this, we introduce Voxtral 3: a new family of state-of-the-art
speech-to-text
and
text-to-speech
models, alongside a voice agent SDK that brings listening, thinking, tool calling, and speaking into a single real-time streaming pipeline.
Voxtral 3 is built to create natural conversational experiences, from contact-center agents and service desks to virtual assistants and hands-free copilots. With support for dozens of languages, expressive speech, complex reasoning, and deep integration with your own tools and data, agents built with Voxtral can interpret nuanced intent, reason through sophisticated requests, and execute multi-step actions across connected systems.
As with other Mistral models, the Voxtral 3 family is designed for sovereign deployment. Available today through Mistral's EU-hosted API or for self-deployment on your own infrastructure, it gives organizations control over where audio is processed, how data is handled, and whether conversations are retained.
Highlights
Accurate transcription across 50+ languages
. The speech-to-text model,
Voxtral Small Transcribe 3
, is state-of-the-art in multilingual transcription, identifying who said what (diarization), using keyphrases to improve its accuracy (context-biasing). It detects when someone has finished speaking so agents can respond without delay (end-of-turn detection).
Natural speech that adapts to the conversation
. The text-to-speech model,
Voxtral Mini TTS 3
, introduces new voices that adjust their emotion, pace, and emphasis based on conversational context, with support for 35+ languages and 50+ dialects.
Human-like intelligence at your (spoken) command
. The new voice agent SDK combines listening, reasoning, tool calling, and speaking into a real-time experience. Agents can retrieve information, interact with business systems, and complete tasks during live conversations over phone or web.
Pro Voice for distinctly accurate voice adaptation
. For professional use cases where basic voice cloning does not suffice, Voxtral’s
Pro Voice
adaptation is able to reproduce a speaker’s distinctive character, emotional range, and speaking style across languages, using just 30 minutes of audio.
Your voice AI, on your infrastructure
. Use Mistral's EU-hosted API or deploy Voxtral 3 on your own infrastructure. Keep sensitive conversations within your environment and control whether any audio is retained.

The voice agent is in control of the whole conversation.
Voxtral 3 introduces a voice agent SDK, built on LiveKit, that runs speech in, reasoning, and speech out as one streaming pipeline. Voxtral Mini Transcribe Realtime 3 listens and detects when the caller has finished speaking, a general model—like
Mistral Small
—reasons and calls tools, and Voxtral Speech 3 replies in the tone of the conversation so far. No stage waits for the one before it, so each turn completes in under one second, and the agent stops talking when the caller cuts in.
During a call, the agent can verify a caller's identity, check a bill, or book an appointment, using function calling, web search, and retrieval over your own systems. It answers and places calls over SIP, runs in browsers and apps over WebRTC, and connects to the telephony, IVR, and contact-center software you already use.
Each stage is a separate model, so you can change the LLM, the voice, or the language without rewriting the agent, and run the whole stack on Mistral's API in Europe or on your own infrastructure.
