Model Information 
Description 
Gemini Omni 1.2 Flash is our next step towards models that can create and edit anything from any input—starting with video. It combines Gemini’s intelligence with our generative media models, representing a leap forward in world understanding, multimodality, and editing. Gemini Omni 1.2 Flash enables high-quality video creation and a more natural way to edit videos through conversation. 
Model dependencies 
Gemini Omni 1.2 Flash is based on Gemini Omni Flash. 
Inputs 
Text strings (e.g., a question, a prompt), images, audio, and video files. 
Outputs 
High-quality, high-resolution video with audio.

Intended Usage and Limitations 
Benefit and Intended Usage 
Gemini Omni 1.2 Flash can be used to generate high-quality, high-resolution videos from any input in a wide range of visual styles. The model is able to faithfully follow simple and complex instructions, simulate real-world physics, and edit videos through conversation.

Frontier Safety Assessment 
Gemini Omni 1.2 Flash is part of the Gemini 3 series of models. To assess Gemini Omni 1.2 Flash for frontier safety, we rely on our evaluations of Gemini 3.1 Pro and Gemini 3.7 Flash as outlined in our Frontier Safety Framework . We found that Gemini 3.1 Pro and Gemini 3.7 Flash did not reach any Tracked or Critical Capability Levels (T/CCLs). Our assessments have shown that Gemini Omni 1.2 Flash does not have meaningful new capabilities or material increases in performance compared to Gemini 3.1 Pro or Gemini 3.7 Flash, therefore based on these results, we are confident that Gemini Omni 1.2 Flash is also unlikely to reach any T/CCLs. 
For more information on our Frontier Safety Assessment, read the Gemini 3.1 Pro Model Card and Gemini 3.7 Flash Model Card . 
Risks and Mitigations 
Similar to our safety approach to our other Gemini models , we built safety at the core of Gemini Omni 1.2 Flash: 
Pre-training mitigations: We used diverse synthetic captioning to improve the model’s ability to accurately represent a wide variety of concepts. 
Post-training mitigations: To maintain high information integrity and prevent unwanted outputs, we deployed advanced production filters. 
Additionally, SynthID watermarks enable users to verify AI-generated content. 
As part of editing videos, Gemini Omni 1.2 Flash is capable of changing people's speech. For now, we are restricting this capability and working to better understand how to safely and responsibly bring it to our users. model card
