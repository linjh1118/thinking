Gemini Omni 1.2 Flash: Fast, cost-effective, and reliable video generation and editing now generally available November 5, 2026 



John Nack 
Sr. Product Manager, Google Cloud 
Today, Gemini Omni 1.2 Flash is generally available on Google Cloud. Built for the everyday videos businesses make in volume — like product videos, virtual try-ons, and ads — this model is fast, cost-effective, and follows your image, video and audio references more closely than before. 
Business users can create with it in Gemini for business and Google Vids, and developers can build with it via API. 
Here's what's new: 
A lower price: $0.07 per second of 720p video, 30% less than Gemini Omni 1.1 Flash, with similar savings at other resolutions. 
Longer clips: Generate and edit videos of up to 15 seconds, up from 10s. 
More consistent results: stronger adherence to image and video references keeps products, characters, and brand details consistent from shot to shot. 
Faster drafts, in higher resolution: create drafts at 360p or 720p, then upscale the best creations to 1080p or 4K. 
More natural speech: You can generate scenes with more fluid, natural-sounding dialogue, clearer lip-sync, and distinct multi-speaker conditioning. 
Editing in conversation: refine the same clip, such as swapping a product, changing lighting, or changing camera angle, instead of starting over. 
Note: Audio references are gated behind allowlisting. 
Gemini Omni 1.2 Flash is ready for production 
Gemini Omni 1.2 Flash is production-ready and backed by Google Cloud’s enterprise-grade infrastructure and security, offering Provisioned Throughput . Outputs carry an imperceptible SynthID watermark.
