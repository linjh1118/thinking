Title: WeVisOne-VL: Unified Visual Perception from Objects to Pixels Authors: Tianming Liang , Junyi Pan , Yuxi Li , Canmiao Fu , Zhipeng Huang , Chong Sun , Chen Li , Jing Lyu View a PDF of the paper titled WeVisOne-VL: Unified Visual Perception from Objects to Pixels, by Tianming Liang and 7 other authors View PDF HTML (experimental) Abstract: Existing vision-language models (VLMs) remain unreliable for fine-grained visual perception. In this report, we present WeVisOne-VL (WeChat Vision All-in-One), a powerful VLM that unifies object-level and pixel-level perception through structured next-token prediction. It supports multiple visual tasks including object detection, referring localization, counting, OCR, segmentation, and matting, enabling localization at any user-specific granularity, from points and boxes to masks and alpha mattes. Architecturally, we integrate a lightweight pixel decoder into the VLM via a special <OBJ> token to generate instance-level pixel outputs. We train the model with a three-stage curriculum SFT followed by mixed-task RL. Across over 30 benchmarks, WeVisOne-VL demonstrates leading performance on both object-level and pixel-level tasks, while maintaining robust rejection for object-absent queries. By releasing WeVisOne-VL to the open-source community, we hope to advance VLMs from broad visual understanding toward truly reliable perception systems for real-world deployment.
