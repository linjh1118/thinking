Molecular dynamics

python
import hyff
sim = hyff.Simulation("system.npz", model="moderato", steps=50000, timestep_fs=1.0,
                      temperature_K=300, tau_ps=0.5, output="run/")
result = sim.run()

or hyff md --model moderato --system system.npz --output run/ --steps 50000.

All options and the system file are checked before any kernel is compiled; an invalid value stops with a one-line message (the CLI prints a traceback only with --debug).

Integrator and engines

Velocity Verlet with RATTLE bond constraints in FP64; forces come from the model's own engine in FP32. presto, vivace and moderato run whole chunks of steps on the GPU, including neighbour-list rebuilds, with CUDA conditional graphs; andante and largo replay captured force graphs per step. chunk (default 100, at most 1,024) is the number of steps per device launch.

The first chunk compiles kernels for the system. Each process can construct at most two compiled engines; use a fresh Python process per simulation.

Thermostat

tau_ps > 0 applies the Bussi–Donadio–Parrinello stochastic velocity rescaling thermostat at temperature_K (> 0) between chunks; tau_ps=0 runs NVE. seed sets the thermostat random stream. The trace in result.json reports the potential energy, temperature and the conserved energy (including the thermostat work) at every chunk.

If an NVT step is rejected by the integrity checks, the run rolls back to the last chunk, redraws velocities and continues for fallback_steps with fallback_timestep_fs; recovery='off' / --no-recovery stops at the first rejected step instead. NVE runs always stop.

Constant pressure (NPT)

python
hyff.Simulation("system.npz", model="moderato", steps=200000, temperature_K=300, tau_ps=0.5,
                pressure_bar=1.0, output="npt/")

or hyff md ... --pressure-bar 1. A Monte Carlo barostat changes the cell between chunks. Every barostat_interval steps (default 100) it proposes a random volume change, scales the cell and the mass-weighted centres of the bonded groups, evaluates the energy once and accepts the move with the Metropolis criterion of the isothermal–isobaric ensemble at temperature_K and pressure_bar. Groups keep their internal geometry, so constraints stay exact, and velocities are unchanged. The largest volume change adapts towards an acceptance rate between 25% and 75%. NPT needs the thermostat (tau_ps > 0) and runs on one GPU.

barostatMoves
isotropic (default)the whole cell; any box shape
semi-isotropicx and y together, or z (membranes); orthorhombic box
anisotropicx, y or z; orthorhombic box

The groups are those of periodic recentering: connected components of constraint_pairs plus bond_pairs, or of the bonds and constraints of molecule_system_xml. Groups split across the box boundary in the input are made whole by lattice translations at the start (the energy is unchanged). For flexible molecules give bond_pairs (or the topology) so that molecules are scaled as wholes; without them every unconstrained atom is scaled on its own, which is equally correct but accepts smaller volume changes (34% instead of 50% acceptance for flexible water).

A move costs one force evaluation and up to two neighbour-list rebuilds, 1–3% of the run time at the default interval (see performance.md). andante and largo rebuild their captured graphs (tens of seconds) when compression outgrows their edge buffers, typically once or twice while the density settles from a low starting value. largo needs the periodic SPME sum (the default for MD systems).

The trace adds volume_A3 and density_g_cm3 at every chunk, and the conserved energy also subtracts the energy change of accepted moves. boxes_A_*.npy holds the cell of every saved frame, result.json reports the barostat under barostat (moves, acceptances, largest volume change, final box) and final_state.npz holds box_A; a restart continues in that cell.

Constraints and flexible MD

constraint_pairs / constraint_distances_A of the system file are held fixed (typically all X–H bonds and rigid water); use 1 fs with ordinary masses. With no constraints (constraint_pairs of shape (0, 2)) the run is flexible: plain velocity Verlet with every bond free. O–H stretches then need a short timestep, 0.5 fs or less. Measured NVE on 306 flexible waters (presto, 0.5 fs, 20 ps): see performance.md. The timestep must be positive and at most 10 fs.

Hydrogen mass repartitioning

  • hmr_mass=3.0 (--hmr-mass 3): every non-water hydrogen gets the given mass; its bonded heavy

atom loses the difference.

  • inclusive_hmr=True (--inclusive-hmr): every hydrogen, including water hydrogens, becomes 3 amu;

heavy atoms compensate and must stay at or above 3 amu.

The heavy partner of each hydrogen comes from constraint_pairs, so HMR needs the X–H constraints. Water is taken from water_idx when present, otherwise every constraint cluster of exactly one O and two H is water. Initial velocities are rescaled for equipartition at the new masses. HMR does not change the timestep.

Restart

python
hyff.Simulation("system.npz", model="moderato", initial_positions="run/final_state.npz", output="run2/")

(--restart run/final_state.npz). Positions, velocities and the cell (box_A, which changes under NPT) continue; step counters, time and the thermostat random stream start anew. final_state.npz is rewritten at every frame save (save_every, default 500 steps) and at the end.

Periodic recentering

recenter_interval_ps (--recenter-interval-ps) translates bonded groups back into the cell at chunk boundaries. The translation is applied in place: the force is periodic, so nothing is recomputed or recaptured, and the next step's neighbour-list check rebuilds the list at the new coordinates (well under a millisecond per event). The saved trajectory and final_state.npz stay unwrapped.

The groups are the connected components of constraint_pairs plus the optional bond_pairs (B, 2) of the system file, or the bonds and constraints of an OpenMM system.xml given as molecule_system_xml / --molecule-topology. Constrained atoms always move together; a molecule whose heavy-atom bonds are not listed is translated in fragments, which leaves the energy unchanged.

largo supports recentering with the periodic SPME long-range sum, the default for MD systems (see largo.md); the isolated sums (dense, tree, native) are refused.

Output

result.json: status, model, engine, checkpoint sha256, engine options, ms_per_step, ns_per_day, wall times, atom/constraint counts, charge and multiplicity, neighbour rebuilds and the energy trace. Frames (frames_*.npy, times_ps_*.npy): the starting state at t = 0, then one frame exactly every save_every steps (chunks end at frame times). Pass debug=True (--debug) to also store engine diagnostics under debug.

Not supported

  • stress or virial output (the Monte Carlo barostat needs neither);
  • NPT on several GPUs;
  • other thermostats (Langevin, Nosé–Hoover) and other integrators;
  • isolated (nonperiodic) MD: the system needs a box; use a box larger than the molecule plus the model cutoff;
  • constraint clusters of more than four atoms or three constraints (e.g. full-bond constraints of a ring).
