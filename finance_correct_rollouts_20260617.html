Deep Dive · q011 · Comparables

单题深拆：Target vs. Walmart 的 FCCR

原始问题完整 rolloutTGTWMTFCCR
原始问题 / Original Question

For the fiscal years 2025 (NYSE: TGT for the fiscal year ended February 1, 2025; NYSE: WMT for the fiscal year ended January 31, 2025), calculate and compare the Fixed Charge Coverage Ratio (FCCR). Use EBITDAR divided by total fixed charges (interest expense plus fixed operating lease cost). For the rent addback, use operating lease cost excluding short-term and variable lease components. Note: $2,347 million for WMT represents the fixed operating lease cost net of variable and short-term components. Which company demonstrates stronger fixed charge coverage, and by how much?

一句话翻译

题目要比较 Target 和 Walmart 在 FY2025 的固定费用覆盖能力：用 EBITDAR 除以总固定费用。固定费用只包括利息和“固定”的经营租赁成本；短期租赁、可变租赁不能算进去。最后判断谁覆盖能力更强、强多少。

1. 这道题到底在考什么？

FCCR，全称 Fixed Charge Coverage Ratio，直译是“固定费用覆盖倍数”。它回答的是：公司经营产生的 earnings-like 能力，能覆盖利息和固定租金这些硬性支出多少倍。公式不是普通 EBITDA，而是 EBITDAR，因为租金本身被当成类似固定融资负担，需要先加回到分子，再放进分母。

FCCR = EBITDAR / Total fixed charges
EBITDAR = Operating income + Depreciation & amortization + Fixed operating lease cost
Total fixed charges = Interest expense + Fixed operating lease cost

这题最容易错在 lease 口径。题目明确要求 rent addback 只用 fixed operating lease cost，不能把 short-term lease cost 和 variable lease cost 混进去。Walmart 的 $2,347M 题目已经提示是 fixed operating lease cost；Target 需要从披露的 operating lease cost 里扣掉 short-term and variable lease costs。

4. 金融小白应该抓住的直觉

不要被 Walmart 的绝对规模吓到。Walmart 的 EBITDAR 远大于 Target，但它的利息和固定租赁成本也大得多。FCCR 看的是“覆盖倍数”，不是绝对利润金额。最后 Target 是 9.84x，Walmart 是 9.73x，所以 Target 略强，大约强 0.12x。

这两个数都很高。一般来说，FCCR 高于 1.0x 才表示分子能覆盖固定费用；很多债权分析会希望看到明显高于 1.5x-2.0x。这里两家公司都接近 10x，说明固定费用压力都不大。题目不是要找“谁危险”，而是要精确比较谁更强。

q002 · General qualitative analysis

Marriott vs. Wyndham Loyalty Programs

MARWHloyalty10-K
中文解释

这题在问什么

它不是泛泛比较哪家酒店更好，而是问两个更具体的问题：第一，客人的住宿需求有多少比例来自会员体系；第二，会员体系靠什么资金机制支撑。最后再判断哪家公司更可能把会员优势转化成长期经济护城河。

Rollout 怎么走

模型先找 Marriott 和 Wyndham 的 FY2025 10-K，解析页面后在 filing 里搜索 loyalty、credit card、funded by 等关键词，再抽出四个核心渗透率和两个资金机制。Marriott 约 75% 美国 room nights、约 68% 全球 room nights 来自会员；Wyndham 超过 53% 美国 check-ins、超过 37% 全球 check-ins 来自会员。Marriott 披露联名信用卡付款是重要资金来源；Wyndham Rewards 则由会员产生的 eligible revenues 向系统内酒店收取贡献资金。

金融小白怎么理解

会员体系的价值不只是“用户多”。它会影响直订比例、获客成本、客户数据、复购和议价能力。Marriott 胜在两个地方：会员渗透率更高，而且联名信用卡让忠诚度计划变成一条更高质量的收入/资金来源，不只是营销成本池。

English counterpart

What the question asks

This is not a generic “which hotel company is better” question. It asks how much demand flows through each loyalty program, how each program is funded, and which company can better turn loyalty into durable economic advantage.

How the rollout works

The model located the FY2025 10-Ks for Marriott and Wyndham, parsed the filings, searched loyalty-related language, and extracted the required metrics. Marriott disclosed about 75% of U.S. room nights and 68% of global room nights from loyalty members. Wyndham disclosed over 53% of U.S. check-ins and over 37% of global check-ins from Wyndham Rewards members. Marriott also disclosed co-branded credit card payments as a significant funding source, while Wyndham Rewards is funded by contributions from eligible revenues collected from system hotels.

Beginner interpretation

A loyalty program matters because it can reduce OTA dependence, increase direct bookings, deepen customer data, and improve repeat behavior. Marriott looks stronger because penetration is higher and the credit-card economics make the program more monetizable and resilient.

q003 · General qualitative analysis

EA vs. Take-Two Competitors

EATTWOcompetitiondisclosure
中文解释

这题在问什么

这是一道披露质量比较题：EA 和 Take-Two 在 10-K 里说自己和谁竞争？有没有提到非游戏娱乐竞争？哪家公司披露得更细？

Rollout 怎么走

模型找到两家公司 FY2025 10-K 的 Business/Competition 部分。EA 主要用泛化表述：主机、PC、移动游戏公司，大型多元化公司，新进入者，以及电影、电视、社交媒体、音乐等非互动娱乐。Take-Two 不仅按类别描述，还点名 EA、Embracer、Playrix、Playtika、Roblox、Savvy Games、Tencent、Ubisoft，以及 Sony、Microsoft、Nintendo 等平台方。

金融小白怎么理解

投资分析里，“披露得细”本身就是信息。更具体的竞争对手名单帮助投资者判断战略压力来自哪里。这里 TTWO 的披露更颗粒化，所以答案是 TTWO 更详细。

English counterpart

What the question asks

This is a disclosure-comparison question: who do EA and Take-Two say they compete with, do they mention non-interactive entertainment, and which company provides more detail?

How the rollout works

The model found each FY2025 10-K and searched the competition disclosure. EA used broad categories such as console, PC, mobile game companies, diversified companies, new entrants, and other entertainment like movies, TV, social media, and music. Take-Two named specific competitors including EA, Embracer, Playrix, Playtika, Roblox, Savvy Games, Tencent, Ubisoft, plus Sony, Microsoft, and Nintendo as platform holders.

Beginner interpretation

Disclosure granularity matters. A named competitor list makes the competitive map more useful to investors. Take-Two’s disclosure is therefore more detailed.

q004 · General quantitative analysis

CRWD vs. PANW Two-Year Revenue CAGR

CRWDPANWCAGRbasis points
CAGR = (Ending revenue / Beginning revenue)^(1 / years) - 1
Basis point difference = percentage-point difference × 10,000
中文解释

这题在问什么

比较 CrowdStrike 和 Palo Alto Networks 从 FY2023 到 FY2025 谁收入增长更快，并把差距换算成 basis points。

Rollout 怎么走

模型用 SEC XBRL company facts 抓两家公司 FY2023 和 FY2025 revenue，再调用 finance calculator 算两年 CAGR。CRWD 从 $2,241.236M 到 $3,953.624M，CAGR 为 32.82%；PANW 从 $6,892.700M 到 $9,221.500M，CAGR 为 15.67%。差距约 1,715 bps。

金融小白怎么理解

CAGR 是“平滑后的年增长率”。PANW 绝对收入更大，但 CRWD 增长速度更快。basis point 是金融里表达百分比差异的常用单位：1% = 100 bps。

English counterpart

What the question asks

Compare which cybersecurity company had the higher revenue growth from FY2023 to FY2025, and express the gap in basis points.

How the rollout works

The model pulled revenue from SEC XBRL company facts and used the finance calculator. CRWD grew from $2,241.236M to $3,953.624M, producing a 32.82% CAGR. PANW grew from $6,892.700M to $9,221.500M, producing a 15.67% CAGR. The gap is about 1,715 basis points.

Beginner interpretation

CAGR is the smoothed annual growth rate. PANW is larger in absolute revenue, but CRWD grew faster. One percent equals 100 basis points.

q006 · General quantitative analysis

Home Depot vs. Lowe's Inventory Efficiency

HDLOWDIOinventory
Average inventory = (Beginning inventory + Ending inventory) / 2
DIO = Average inventory / Cost of sales × Actual fiscal days
中文解释

这题在问什么

比较 Home Depot 和 Lowe’s FY2024 谁库存周转更快。核心指标是 DIO，也就是 Days Inventory Outstanding。

Rollout 怎么走

模型从 filings 和 XBRL facts 抽 cost of sales、期初/期末 inventory，并注意 FY2024 实际天数不同：HD 是 371 天，LOW 是 364 天。HD 平均库存 $22,213.5M，DIO 77.60 天；LOW 平均库存 $17,151.5M，DIO 111.89 天。HD 快约 34.29 天。

金融小白怎么理解

DIO 可以粗略理解为库存平均要放多少天才卖掉。越低通常越好，因为现金更少被压在库存里。HD 的 DIO 低很多，说明它把库存变成销售的速度更快。

English counterpart

What the question asks

Compare Home Depot and Lowe’s inventory efficiency for FY2024 using Days Inventory Outstanding, or DIO.

How the rollout works

The model extracted cost of sales, beginning inventory, ending inventory, and actual fiscal-year days. HD used 371 days; LOW used 364 days. HD average inventory was $22,213.5M and DIO was 77.60 days. LOW average inventory was $17,151.5M and DIO was 111.89 days. HD turns inventory about 34.29 days faster.

Beginner interpretation

DIO estimates how many days inventory sits before sale. Lower is usually better because less cash is trapped in inventory. HD is more efficient on this metric.

TGTWMTFCCRleases
EBITDAR = Operating income + D&A + Fixed operating lease cost
Total fixed charges = Interest expense + Fixed operating lease cost

中文解释

这题在问什么

比较 Target 和 Walmart 的固定费用覆盖能力。FCCR 看的是公司 earnings-like 的金额能覆盖利息和固定租金多少倍。

这不是说 Target 整体一定比 Walmart 强，而是说在这个固定费用覆盖指标上 Target 略高。两家公司都远高于一般安全阈值，差距只有约 0.12x。

How the rollout works

The model extracted operating income, D&A, interest expense, and fixed operating lease cost. The lease nuance mattered: Target’s reported operating lease cost included short-term and variable components, while Walmart’s $2,347M was already fixed operating lease cost. TGT FCCR was 9.84x; WMT FCCR was 9.73x.

Beginner interpretation

The result does not mean Target is broadly superior. It means Target is slightly stronger on this specific fixed-charge coverage metric, while both companies show very strong coverage.

q013 · Precedents / M&A

Baker Hughes Buying Chart Industries

BKRGTLSpremiumsynergies
Premium = Offer price / unaffected share price - 1
Synergy % of revenue = run-rate synergies / target revenue
Synergy % of SG&A = run-rate synergies / target SG&A
中文解释

这题在问什么

这是 M&A precedent analysis：算收购溢价、看组合后业务 mix 怎么变、把成本协同放到 Chart 的收入和 SG&A 里衡量，并解释为什么 BKR 会买。

Rollout 怎么走

模型查 BKR 的 8-K 展示材料、Chart merger proxy、Chart FY2024 财务数据和股价数据。Offer price 是 $210；Chart 7/28/2025 unaffected close 是 $171.65；premium = 22.34%。BKR IET 的 non-O&G revenue mix 从约 15% 到约 30%。Run-rate cost synergies 是 $325M，占 Chart FY2024 revenue 的 7.81%，占 SG&A 的 59.37%。

金融小白怎么理解

M&A 题通常有三层：交易条款、市场价格、战略逻辑。溢价说明买方多付多少；协同占收入/SG&A 的比例用于判断管理层目标有多激进；业务 mix 变化解释这笔交易为什么有战略意义。

English counterpart

What the question asks

This is an M&A precedent analysis: calculate the premium, assess the pro forma mix shift, size cost synergies against Chart’s revenue and SG&A, and explain Baker Hughes’ strategic rationale.

How the rollout works

The model used BKR 8-K exhibits, Chart’s merger proxy, Chart FY2024 financials, and market price data. The offer price was $210, the unaffected close on 7/28/2025 was $171.65, and the premium was 22.34%. BKR’s IET non-O&G revenue mix increased from about 15% to about 30%. The $325M synergy target equaled 7.81% of Chart FY2024 revenue and 59.37% of SG&A.

Beginner interpretation

M&A questions combine deal terms, market data, and strategic logic. Premium measures how much extra the buyer pays; synergy percentages test whether management’s target is modest or aggressive; mix shift explains why the deal matters strategically.

q014 · Precedents / M&A

IonQ Buying SkyWater

IONQSKYTmultiplesquantum
Transaction value / revenue = transaction value / FY revenue
Transaction value / adjusted EBITDA = transaction value / FY adjusted EBITDA
中文解释

这题在问什么

判断 IonQ 收购 SkyWater 的估值到底像普通半导体代工、战略性本土制造，还是量子计算垂直整合溢价。

Rollout 怎么走

模型查 merger announcement、SkyWater FY2025 results 和资产负债表。用 $1.8B equity value、FY2025 revenue $442.1M、adjusted EBITDA $53.2M 算出 equity-value/revenue 约 4.07x，equity-value/adjusted EBITDA 约 33.83x；加 net debt 后 EV/EBITDA 约 37.60x。

金融小白怎么理解

成熟 foundry 通常不会靠 12% 左右 EBITDA margin 拿到三十多倍 EBITDA。这个价格更多反映 IonQ 想把量子芯片制造能力纳入自身路线图的战略期权价值。本土制造重要，但不是最主要解释。

English counterpart

What the question asks

Determine whether IonQ’s acquisition of SkyWater is priced like a normal semiconductor foundry, a domestic manufacturing asset, or a quantum vertical-integration premium.

How the rollout works

The model gathered the merger announcement, SkyWater FY2025 results, and balance sheet data. Using $1.8B equity value, FY2025 revenue of $442.1M, and adjusted EBITDA of $53.2M, it calculated about 4.07x revenue and 33.83x adjusted EBITDA. Including net debt, EV/adjusted EBITDA was about 37.60x.

Beginner interpretation

A low-margin mature foundry would not normally trade at a mid-30s EBITDA multiple. The valuation mainly reflects IonQ’s strategic desire to own manufacturing capability for its quantum roadmap. Domestic manufacturing value supports the deal, but the dominant driver is quantum vertical integration.

q015 · Precedents / M&A

Pfizer Buying Seagen

PFESGENenterprise valueEV revenue
Equity value = offer price per share × shares outstanding
Enterprise value = equity value + debt - cash and short-term investments
EV / revenue = enterprise value / FY revenue
中文解释

这题在问什么

用 Pfizer 收购 Seagen 的现金报价和 Seagen FY2022 数据，计算 equity value、enterprise value 和 EV/FY2022 revenue multiple。

Rollout 怎么走

模型用 2023-03-13 SGEN 8-K 找 $229/share 交易对价，用 FY2022 10-K cover page 找 186,789,367 股，用资产负债表找 cash、short-term investments 和 debt，用收入表找 FY2022 revenue。结果：equity value 约 $42.77B；现金和短投约 $1.735B；长期债务 $0；EV 约 $41.04B；EV/revenue 约 20.91x。

金融小白怎么理解

Equity value 是股东拿到的钱；enterprise value 更接近买经营性业务的价格，因为要加债、扣现金。Seagen 有很多现金且没有长期债，所以 EV 小于 equity value。

English counterpart

What the question asks

Use Pfizer’s cash offer and Seagen FY2022 data to calculate implied equity value, enterprise value, and EV/FY2022 revenue.

How the rollout works

The model used the March 13, 2023 SGEN 8-K for the $229/share consideration, the FY2022 10-K cover page for 186,789,367 shares, the balance sheet for cash, short-term investments, and debt, and the income statement for FY2022 revenue. Equity value was about $42.77B, enterprise value was about $41.04B, and EV/revenue was about 20.91x.

Beginner interpretation

Equity value is what shareholders receive. Enterprise value adjusts for debt and cash to approximate the price of the operating business. Because Seagen had substantial cash and no long-term debt, EV was below equity value.

Kraft Heinz Adjusted EBITDA and Cash-Adjusted Margin
